What changes at 100 units?

PCB and assembly setup fees spread across the batch, components receive quantity discounts, and freight is shared. Bulk quotes for the LCD, Luckfox and camera could also reduce the largest portion of the parts budget. We do not yet have a reviewed 100-unit production quote.
