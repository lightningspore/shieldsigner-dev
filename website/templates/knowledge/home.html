FIRMWARE ON REAL HARDWARE

LVGL screens. Rust camera. Original signing logic.

The current development image boots on Luckfox Mini: bench testing confirmed the LVGL interface and camera QR scanning. Python still owns the wallet workflow; native LVGL draws the screens, Rust controls the camera, and ZXing-C++ decodes QR pixels.

Tested bench image

lvgl-dev.cb6170a37a5a uses the standard 240×240 display/button board. Its result does not qualify the separate 2.8-inch pill carrier.

Measured, with boundaries

Packaged-runtime tests observed Python at 11,012 KiB at Home and 13,576 KiB during preview, counting private anonymous RAM plus logical swap. These are workload observations, not total device RAM.

What remains

Public-fixture signing and export checks pass. Real-wallet end-to-end acceptance is pending; carrier and smartcard qualification remain separate.
