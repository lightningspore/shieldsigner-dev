From the repository root:

```sh
mise run baremetal:check
mise run baremetal:app:autostart:dut1:build
mise run baremetal:app:sd:prepare
```

These commands create regular build files; they do not flash a card. `baremetal/build/app-sd.img` is mutable build output. For a reviewed flash, copy image, manifests and ELF into an immutable checkpoint and verify their hashes first. Generated source adaptations should be changed through their `tools/prepare_*.py` generators and provenance, preserving upstream attribution. Do not commit OS caches, private infrastructure, private wallet data or camera captures.

## Bench and operating constraints

DUT1 is the RV1103 Mini A with no NAND and the LCD/button PCB plus SC3336 camera, USB serial `rv1103-dut1` (normally `/dev/cu.usbmodemrv1103_dut11`). With SD installed, USB reset boots the card rather than entering maskrom. Do not interrupt or reset it while the user is operating the UI.

DUT0 is a separate RV1103 Mini A with a loopback jumper installed. Do not load `app-ui`, `mini-pcb-ui` or `usb-only` onto DUT0. REF is a Luckfox Pico, not a Mini; preserve its Linux installation. REF/ADB was unavailable at handoff. No current device connection state is implied by these descriptions.

LCD pins: CS48, SCK49, MOSI50, reset51, DC52, backlight43. Buttons: Up57, Down59, Left56, Right54, center58, KEY1 55, KEY2 global4, KEY3 53. Use the documented Mini PCB profile rather than older fixture wiring.

Never allocate in IRQ context or let MicroPython NLR cross C++/Rust frames. Keep borrowed camera-buffer ownership explicit. Do not save private camera frames, entropy, mnemonic words or wallet data into receipts or logs.
